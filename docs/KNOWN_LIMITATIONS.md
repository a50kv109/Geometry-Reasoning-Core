# Known limitations

- The package is research software; no formal proof of implementation correctness is included.
- The independent verifier covers selected 2D reflection properties, not every operation or domain.
- Experimental branch selection and 3D tetrahedron handling are not universal solvers.
- The test suites exercise finite fixtures and do not establish correctness for all coordinate scales or degenerate inputs.
- Physical paper thickness, continuous folding kinematics, and general 3D collision detection are outside scope.
- The license copyright holder is not confirmed; owner confirmation is required before publication.
